# PoseKi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

PoseKi analyzes photos on your device to provide pose guidance. PoseKi does not upload your photos or pose landmarks to our servers.

## Information processed

- Camera photos and pose landmarks are processed locally on your device.
- Pose session history and settings are stored locally on your device.
- If you purchase a subscription, Apple and RevenueCat process the transaction and may receive the identifiers required to manage the purchase. PoseKi does not receive your payment card details.

## Sharing

Pose photos and pose landmarks are not sold or shared by PoseKi. We do not use them for advertising or automated profiling.

## Retention and deletion

Local pose history can be deleted from within the app or by deleting PoseKi from your device. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, visit the PoseKi support page:

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will always be published in this repository.
