# PoseKi Support

PoseKi is an on-device photo pose coach. It analyzes one person in a photo and suggests improvements to alignment, head tilt, shoulders, arms, and symmetry.

## Contact

For help with PoseKi, open a GitHub issue:
https://github.com/HectorTa1989/Poseki-public-URL/issues

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, PoseKi version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not attach private photos unless you have removed any personal information.

## Common questions

**Does PoseKi upload my photos?**  
No. Photo and pose analysis run on the device.

**Why did PoseKi not analyze my photo?**  
PoseKi currently supports one clearly visible person per shot. Try better lighting, keep the full body in frame, and avoid multiple people.

**How do I manage a subscription?**  
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID subscription settings.
